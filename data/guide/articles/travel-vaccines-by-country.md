These pages are the destination guides in English for trips that **leave from Portugal**. A direct flight from Lisbon or Porto is not the same as arriving from a yellow fever country, and that is the detail most generic checklists miss.

The video [travel consultation](/marcar/travel) is in English (€39). The prescription is a Portuguese electronic prescription. Yellow fever, when it is needed, is given only at an [International Vaccination Centre](/blog/international-vaccination-centres-portugal) — [not at CUF](/blog/vacina-febre-amarela-cuf).

## Where to be vaccinated after the consultation

The jab, when it is yellow fever or another travel vaccine given on the international circuit, is at an [International Vaccination Centre in Portugal](/blog/international-vaccination-centres-portugal). [Yellow fever](/blog/vacina-febre-amarela-guia-completo) certificate is valid 10 days after the dose. City notes (in Portuguese): [Lisbon](/blog/vacinas-viajante-lisboa), [Porto](/blog/vacinas-viajante-porto), [Coimbra](/blog/vacinas-viajante-coimbra), [Braga](/blog/vacinas-viajante-braga), [Algarve](/blog/vacinas-viajante-algarve).

If your vaccine record is from another country, bring it. The consultation checks what is documented.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which vaccines do I need to travel from Portugal?

It depends on the itinerary, not only the country, and not on whether you are Portuguese. There are vaccines required for entry (often yellow fever), vaccines recommended because of risk (hepatitis A, typhoid), and routine vaccines. The travel consultation sets the plan and issues the prescription for the vaccination centre.

### Where do I get the vaccines in Portugal?

At International Vaccination Centres, with a prescription. Lisbon, Porto, Coimbra, Braga and the Algarve each have a guide. CUF does not give the yellow fever circuit.

### I live in Portugal but I am not Portuguese. Is this the same?

Yes. These pages assume you are leaving from Portugal. The consultation is in English. The prescription is a Portuguese electronic prescription, sent by SMS or email.
