In Mozambique the main health concern is not yellow fever. It is **malaria** — present across the whole country, including Maputo and the beaches. Yellow fever depends mostly on something many people do not think about: **where you stop over**. That is the question for anyone flying out of Portugal, including foreign residents.

A [travel consultation](/marcar/travel) (€39, in English) reads the route, stopovers included, and prescribes malaria tablets when they are indicated.

## Quick summary

- **Required on entry (direct flight from Portugal):** no vaccine.
- **Yellow fever:** required if you arrive from a yellow fever risk country, **including stopovers of more than 12 hours** in those countries. The CDC does not recommend it for a trip to Mozambique itself.
- **Malaria:** risk <strong>country-wide</strong>. The CDC recommends preventive tablets.
- **Other vaccines to consider:** hepatitis A, typhoid, hepatitis B, rabies.
- **When to book:** 4 to 8 weeks ahead.

## Yellow fever: it all depends on the stopover

Mozambique requires a yellow fever certificate for travellers aged 1 year or older who arrive from countries with a risk of transmission — and that includes **airport stopovers in those countries of more than 12 hours**.

In practice:

- **Direct Lisbon–Maputo flight:** you do not need it.
- **A flight with a long stopover in Luanda, Addis Ababa, Nairobi or another African risk country:** you may need it. Check how long the stopover is.
- **A combined trip** (for example [Angola](/blog/travel-vaccines-angola) and Mozambique): you need it.

If you need it, the vaccine is given at an **International Vaccination Centre** and must be done **at least 10 days before** arrival. [See the centres in Portugal](/blog/international-vaccination-centres-portugal). By city: [Lisbon](/blog/vacina-febre-amarela-lisboa), [Porto](/blog/vacina-febre-amarela-porto). Vaccine guide: [yellow fever](/blog/vacina-febre-amarela-guia-completo). [It is not given at CUF](/blog/vacina-febre-amarela-cuf).

## Malaria: risk everywhere

Malaria exists in every region of Mozambique and is mainly *Plasmodium falciparum*, the most severe form. The parasites are resistant to chloroquine. The CDC recommends preventive tablets with one of these options: atovaquone-proguanil, doxycycline, mefloquine or tafenoquine.

That also applies to:

- **Maputo** and other cities;
- **Beaches and islands** (Tofo, Vilankulo, Bazaruto, Ilha de Moçambique);
- **Parks and reserves.**

The choice of medicine is medical and needs a prescription: [malaria prophylaxis](/consulta/profilaxia-malaria).

As well as the medicine:

- A suitable repellent, especially in the late afternoon and at night.
- An insecticide-treated net, or a room with air conditioning.
- **Any fever during the trip or in the months after you return is an emergency.** Always tell the doctor you were in Mozambique.

## Other vaccines to consider

- **Hepatitis A:** for most travellers.
- **Typhoid:** family visits, rural areas, long stays.
- **Hepatitis B:** long stays, healthcare work, or a chance of medical treatment on the ground.
- **Rabies:** long stays, outdoor activities, contact with animals.
- **Cholera:** only in specific situations (for example humanitarian work in an outbreak area).
- **Routine vaccines:** measles and tetanus in particular. If your record is from outside Portugal, bring it.

More context: [malaria and hepatitis A/B](/blog/malaria-hepatite-a-b-viajante).

## How far ahead to book

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| 6–8 weeks before | Travel consultation and a vaccine plan |
| At least 10 days before | Yellow fever vaccine, if your stopover requires it |
| 1–2 weeks before | Collect malaria tablets |
| Last minute | Still worth it: malaria tablets can be prescribed in time |

The [online consultation](/marcar/travel) is how you settle the stopovers and the prescription before you go to a centre, if yellow fever is actually needed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need yellow fever vaccine for Mozambique?

Only if you arrive from a yellow fever risk country, or you stop over there for more than 12 hours.

### I am only going to Maputo and the beaches. Do I need malaria tablets?

Yes. The risk exists across the whole country, including cities and beach areas.

### I am Mozambican and I live in Portugal. I am visiting family. Do I still need this?

Yes. Time living outside a malaria area reduces the partial protection people sometimes assume they still have, and the entry rules apply the same way.
